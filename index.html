How Relay AI Works

Submit → Review → Match → Connect

Four steps. You only have to do the first one.

    Tell us what you need

    Submit your basic contact details and select whether you're looking for Development or Marketing.

  2. 02

    We review your requirement

    The Relay AI team reviews your enquiry — a person, not just a form in a database.

  3. 03

    We find the right partner

    Relay AI identifies a relevant partner from its network based on what you actually need.

  4. 04

    We connect you

    The relevant partner's details are shared with you. From there, it's your conversation.
