Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

1. Who we are

Relay AI (“Relay AI”, “we”, “us”) helps businesses connect with relevant development and marketing service providers. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website, why we collect it, and what we do with it.

Relay AI is operated by Relay AI, 142-A, New IT Park Building, First Floor, Electronic Complex, Pardesipura, Indore, Madhya Pradesh 452010, India. For any question about this policy or your data, contact us at mehul.n@hiphype.co.

2. Information we collect

We only collect what you give us and the minimum technical data needed to run the site.

Information you submit

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number, including the country code you select
  • Requirement type — whether you need Development or Marketing
  • Message — optional free text, up to 500 characters

Information collected automatically

  • IP address and the date and time of your submission, kept to prevent spam and abuse of the form

We do not use advertising trackers, analytics profiling, or third-party marketing pixels on this website.

3. How we use your information

  • To review your enquiry and understand what you are looking for
  • To identify a relevant partner from our network
  • To contact you about your enquiry and, where relevant, to arrange a call
  • To protect the form against spam and abuse

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not add you to marketing lists without your consent.

4. Sharing your information with partners

This is the most important thing to understand about our service. When we identify a relevant partner for your requirement, we share your enquiry details — including your name, email address, phone number, requirement type and message — with that partner so they can contact you.

By submitting the form, you are asking us to make that introduction and you consent to this sharing. We share your details only with partners we consider relevant to your stated requirement. Once a partner receives your details, their own privacy policy governs how they handle your information, and any agreement you enter into is between you and them.

If you would prefer that we review your enquiry without sharing your details, tell us in the message field or contact us at mehul.n@hiphype.co.

5. Other parties who process data for us

  • Railway — our hosting and database provider, which stores the site and your enquiry record
  • An email provider, if enabled, used solely to notify our team that a new enquiry has arrived

These providers process data on our instructions and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry records for as long as needed to make an introduction and to keep a record of the connections we have made, and no longer than 24 months after your last contact with us, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

7. Cookies

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. The only cookie we set is a sign-in cookie on our private admin area, which is used by our own team and never by visitors to this site.

8. Security

Enquiries are transmitted over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and stored in a private database. Access to enquiry data is restricted to our team through a password-protected admin area. No system can be guaranteed completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

9. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you
  • correct anything that is inaccurate
  • delete your enquiry and the personal data in it
  • stop sharing your details with partners
  • withdraw consent you have previously given

To make any of these requests, email mehul.n@hiphype.co. We will respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.

10. Children

This website is intended for businesses and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Please check back from time to time.
